## Top risks (score ≥ 12) and response plan

1. **R2 Prefill latency (16) and R24 cache reuse (12):** P2 measures warm vs. cold TTFT and every cache-defeating mechanism before any feature depends on them. Alternative engines are tested before a model is rejected.
2. **R1 Turkish quality (15) and R4 memory pollution (15):** these decide whether LAZIS feels like "someone who knows me". Both have hard gates. Blind ratings by the user are decisive for R1; calibration and review for R4.
3. **R28 policy calibration and R27 confirmation fatigue:** together they decide whether autonomous learning feels natural and stays trustworthy. P9 measures both and adjusts the policy only by ADR.
4. **R5 identity, R25 document trust and R26 research privacy (12):** each has a zero-tolerance gate (no name-only confirmed links, no trust-cap violations, no private query auto-sent).
5. **R3 TTS, R6 memory, R9 injection, R13 scope (12):** each has a named spike or gate in the roadmap.
